Before filming begins, production counsel must secure Errors and Omissions insurance. Vetting a screenplay for trademark dilution, character defamation, and title collisions traditionally takes 5 to 10 business days — and one missed landmine halts financing or triggers costly litigation.

This is Greenlight — an autonomous pre-production clearance copilot built for the Google Cloud Blockbuster Hackathon. Powered by the Google Agent Development Kit and Parallel's Search M-C-P, Greenlight turns that multi-day bottleneck into an auditable clearance dossier in under a minute.

Let's run a live audit on an encumbered script: The Apprentice's Revenge. It features an encumbered title, a real firearm in a crime scene, a luxury watch, and an unreserved dialogue phone number.

Under the hood, Stage 1 uses Google A-D-K's Sequential Agent to parse entities. Stage 2 fans out three specialist agents concurrently with A-D-K's Parallel Agent — Title, Brand, and Character clearance — each querying live trademark filings, Wikipedia, and news registries in real time via Parallel's Search M-C-P.

In seconds, the clearance team surfaces four real exposures.

Title Clearance flags a HIGH reverse-confusion risk under Lanham Act Section 43-A, citing the 2024 film The Apprentice, and suggests pre-cleared alternative titles.

Brand Clearance catches two issues: a Glock 19 in a criminal assault — trademark tarnishment under Lanham Act Section 43-C — and a Rolex Submariner in unlicensed hero placement. For the firearm, Greenlight pulls a vetted fictional replacement straight from its greeking catalog, giving art departments a usable alternative.

Prop Clearance inspects the dialogue phone number: four one five, five five five, zero two five zero, and catches that it falls outside the federally reserved fictional block — the difference between a safe prop and a real subscriber line.

Crucially, Greenlight also avoids false alarms. Character Clearance screens protagonist Julian Drake under the Restatement Second of Torts, finds zero living collisions, and affirmatively clears the name. A tool that flags everything is useless — Greenlight knows the difference.

To keep scoring auditable rather than an L-L-M guess, Greenlight computes risk with a fixed formula: 100, minus 25 per high-severity flag, 10 per medium, 3 per low. Anyone can verify the math against the flags.

One click on Export Clearance Memo generates an institutional insurance memo formatted with underlying case law, ready for an insurer's review.

Greenlight combines Google A-D-K multi-agent orchestration, Gemini, and Parallel's Search M-C-P to deliver fast, source-cited legal triage — making human clearance faster, not replacing it. Thank you.
