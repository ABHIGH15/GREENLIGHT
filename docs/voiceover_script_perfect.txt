Before filming begins, production counsel must secure Errors and Omissions insurance. Vetting a screenplay for trademark dilution, character defamation, and title collisions traditionally takes 5 to 10 business days — and one missed landmine halts financing or triggers litigation.

This is Greenlight — an autonomous pre-production clearance copilot built for the Google Cloud Blockbuster Hackathon. Powered by the Google Agent Development Kit and Parallel's Search MCP, Greenlight turns that multi-day bottleneck into an auditable clearance dossier in under a minute.

Let's run a live audit on an encumbered script: The Apprentice's Revenge. It features an encumbered title, a real firearm in a crime scene, a luxury watch, and an unreserved dialogue phone number.

Under the hood, Stage 1 uses Google ADK's SequentialAgent to parse entities. Stage 2 fans out three specialist agents concurrently with ADK's ParallelAgent — Title, Brand, and Character clearance — each querying live trademark filings, Wikipedia, and news registries in real time via Parallel's Search MCP.

In seconds, the clearance team surfaces four real exposures.

Title Clearance flags a HIGH reverse-confusion risk under Lanham Act Section 43(a), citing the 2024 film The Apprentice, and suggests pre-cleared alternative titles.

Brand Clearance catches two issues: a Glock 19 in a criminal assault — trademark tarnishment under Lanham Act Section 43(c) — and a Rolex Submariner in unlicensed hero placement. For the firearm, Greenlight pulls a vetted fictional replacement straight from its greeking catalog, giving art departments a usable alternative.

Prop Clearance inspects the dialogue phone number and catches that it falls outside the federally reserved fictional block — the difference between a safe prop and a real subscriber line.

Crucially, Greenlight also avoids false alarms. Character Clearance screens protagonist Julian Drake under the Restatement Second of Torts, finds zero living collisions, and affirmatively clears the name. A tool that flags everything is useless — Greenlight knows the difference.

To keep scoring auditable rather than an LLM guess, Greenlight computes risk with a fixed formula: 100, minus 25 per high-severity flag, 10 per medium, 3 per low. Anyone can verify the math against the flags.

One click on Export Clearance Memo generates an institutional E&O memo formatted with underlying case law, ready for an insurer's review.

Greenlight combines Google ADK multi-agent orchestration, Gemini, and Parallel's Search MCP to deliver fast, source-cited legal triage — making human clearance faster, not replacing it. Thank you.
