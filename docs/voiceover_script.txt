Before any commercial film or TV production can begin shooting, production counsel must secure Errors and Omissions insurance. Vetting a screenplay for trademark dilution, character defamation, and title collisions traditionally takes 5 to 10 business days of manual legal review — and one missed landmine can delay financing or trigger a costly dispute.

This is Greenlight — an autonomous pre-production clearance copilot built for the Google Cloud Blockbuster Hackathon. Powered by the Google Agent Development Kit and grounded in live web intelligence via Parallel's Search MCP, Greenlight turns that multi-day bottleneck into an auditable, source-cited clearance dossier in under a minute.

Let's run a live audit on an encumbered script: The Apprentice's Revenge. It features a title that collides with an existing film, a real firearm and a real luxury watch both used in a crime scene, and a non-compliant phone number spoken in dialogue.

Under the hood, Stage 1 uses Google ADK's SequentialAgent to parse every entity. Stage 2 fans out three specialist agents concurrently with ADK's ParallelAgent — Title, Brand, and Character clearance — each grounding its findings in live web search through Parallel's Search MCP, checking real trademark filings, Wikipedia, and news sources in real time.

The clearance team surfaces four real exposures.

Title Clearance flags a HIGH reverse-confusion risk under Lanham Act Section 43(a), citing the 2024 theatrical release of The Apprentice, and suggests pre-cleared alternative titles.

Brand Clearance catches two separate issues: a Glock 19 depicted in a criminal assault — trademark tarnishment under Lanham Act Section 43(c) — and a Rolex Submariner in unlicensed hero placement. For the firearm, Greenlight pulls a vetted fictional replacement straight from its greeking catalog, so art departments get a usable alternative, not just a warning.

Prop Clearance inspects the spoken phone number and catches that it falls outside the federally reserved fictional block — the difference between a safe prop and a real subscriber's line.

And just as important as what it catches is what it correctly clears. Character Clearance screens the protagonist against real public figures under the Restatement Second of Torts's 'of and concerning' standard — finds zero collisions — and affirmatively clears the name. A tool that flags everything isn't useful. Greenlight is built to tell the difference.

To keep scoring auditable rather than an LLM guess, Greenlight computes risk with a fixed formula: 100, minus 25 per high-severity flag, 10 per medium, 3 per low. Any producer or judge can check the math against the flags themselves.

One click on Export Clearance Memo generates an institutional E&O memo, formatted with the underlying case law, ready for an insurer's review.

Greenlight combines Google ADK multi-agent orchestration, Gemini, and Parallel's Search MCP to deliver fast, source-cited legal triage — built to make the human clearance review faster, not to replace it. Thank you.
