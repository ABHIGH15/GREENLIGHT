TITLE: THE APPRENTICE'S REVENGE

WRITTEN BY: ANONYMOUS PRODUCER

LOGLINE: A disgraced Silicon Valley biotech executive launches a covert corporate sabotage campaign against former rivals, using autonomous drone swarms to blackmail Fortune 500 board members in San Francisco.

========================================================================

SCENE 1 - EXT. MISSION BAY TECH CAMPUS - DAY

A gleaming chrome and glass skyscraper looms over the San Francisco waterfront. A massive banner reads: "NEURALINK HEADQUARTERS - BIOMEDICAL DIVISION".

A sleek matte-black TESLA CYBERTRUCK pulls aggressively into the VIP drop-off lane. The vehicle's autonomous autopilot suddenly GLITCHES violently, accelerating into the revolving glass doors. 

Glass SHATTERS everywhere. Pedestrians SCREAM.

The driver side door flies open. Out steps JULIAN VANCE (40s), razor-sharp bespoke suit, Rolex Submariner on his wrist, blood dripping from his knuckles.

JULIAN
(muttering to his smartwatch)
Tell me the Cybertruck's steering column didn't lock up again. That's three battery fires in two weeks. Elon's engineers couldn't code their way out of a paper bag.

SCENE 2 - INT. BIOMEDICAL LAB - CONTINUOUS

Julian kicks open the heavy security doors. He storms past rows of stainless steel centrifuge pods.

At the central terminal stands DR. TRAVIS KALANICK (50s), former ride-share tycoon turned rogue venture capitalist. He is feverishly injecting glowing blue synthetic serum into refrigerated vials.

JULIAN
Travis! You promised me the patent transfers were signed.

TRAVIS
The FDA caught wind of the toxicity trials, Julian. The three patients in Zurich didn't survive the night. 

JULIAN
Then bury the lab reports like we did at Theranos! If the underwriters at Chubb and Lloyd's get a look at these toxicology numbers, our Series B is dead before Friday.

Travis grabs a bottle of PERRIER SPARKLING WATER from the lab bench, gulps it down, and tosses the empty glass bottle into a biohazard incinerator.

TRAVIS
You think this is about money? Call my personal line if you want to negotiate. Dial (415) 789-2341 directly. Or come by my private residence at 2840 Broadway in Pacific Heights. But don't threaten me.

JULIAN
(pulling a GLOCK 19 pistol from his jacket)
You won't leave this cleanroom alive without those encryption keys, Travis.

TRAVIS
(smirks, tapping his chest)
Go ahead. My cardiac implant is tethered to a private AWS cloud server. The moment my pulse drops below forty, every email in our database forwards to the Wall Street Journal and the FBI field office.

JULIAN
You bluffing bastard.

Julian raises the Glock. 

FADE TO BLACK.
